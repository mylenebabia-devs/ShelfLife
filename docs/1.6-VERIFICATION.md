# ShelfLife 1.6 — Navigation, cards, photos and Settings

- Home / Inventory / Alerts / Profile labels remain visible in a bar with content-driven height and explicit text styling.
- Product Details and Add stock buttons use equal heights, widths, padding and single-line labels.
- Details opens a wider scrollable panel with separated identifiers, stock information, photo and existing actions.
- Profile → Settings contains Edit account & photo, Notification settings, and Default inventory sort. Sort preference is saved per staff member and applied when opening Inventory.
- New product and Edit product forms support optional Choose photo, preview and Remove photo. Save commits the choice; Cancel leaves the saved product unchanged. Photos apply to all batches of a SKU and survive archive/delete/restore. Add stock inherits the existing product photo. No photo is required: category icons remain the fallback.
- Photos are copied into private on-device storage, limited to supported images under 15 MB / 60 megapixels, sampled and re-encoded. This is a local photo import, not a cloud upload. Existing account photos and records remain intact. Unsaved form drafts can be lost on rotation.
