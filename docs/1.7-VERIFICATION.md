# ShelfLife 1.7 — Barcode lookup and Profile cleanup

Camera and manual barcode lookup check saved inventory first. An existing product opens its editable registration details; use Details → Add stock afterward to receive another delivery. Unknown barcodes open registration and automatically query Open Food Facts after a short typing pause. The barcode field inside Add/Edit also triggers lookup when changed or scanned.

Available name, brand, category, description and pack size fill empty fields. All suggestions remain editable; late results do not overwrite staff input. Changing a barcode clears unchanged suggestions from the previous lookup. Missing records, unsupported barcode formats, timeouts and offline conditions display a message and keep manual entry available. A numeric EAN/UPC/GTIN of 8 or 12–14 digits is eligible for online lookup; leading zeros stay intact. Existing SKU and stock unit remain fixed to protect ledger history. No expiry date, quantity or receiving date is inferred from catalog data. Photos remain optional local imports.

Profile contains the staff identity card, Account information (name/email/phone/photo), Change password, Settings and Logout. Settings contains Notification settings, Default inventory sort, Reports & stock history, Load demo groceries, and Product lookup & data. No necessary store tools were removed. Password changes require the current password, matching new passwords and at least eight characters; the hash uses a fresh salt.

## Catalog source and privacy

Only the barcode is sent to https://world.openfoodfacts.org/api/v2/product/{barcode}; staff information, local quantities and photos are not sent. HTTPS, response-size limits, connection/read timeouts, a dedicated network executor and a bounded session cache are used. Catalog availability/coverage is not guaranteed. Categories may require staff correction. Source attribution is displayed alongside suggestions and under Settings.

Data: Open Food Facts contributors, Open Database License (ODbL). https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use
API documentation: https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/v2/products/get-product-by-code/
The app identifies itself with a ShelfLife User-Agent; set a maintained project contact in BarcodeLookup.java before public release. Review ODbL attribution/share-alike requirements before distributing a database combining imported catalog data.
